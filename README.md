Privacy Policy
German ZKF Manager · Last updated: November 14, 2026

What the app accesses
With the account owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the app may access basic profile information (display name and avatar), account statistics (follower, following and like counts), and the owner's public video list.

How the information is used
This information is used only to show the owner their account performance and to publish content the owner chooses to post. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Storage
The app does not keep a database of personal data. Access tokens are handled by the authorization provider and used only to carry out the actions the owner requests.

Third parties
The app connects to TikTok's APIs. TikTok's own Privacy Policy applies to data held by TikTok.

Revoking access
You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services.

Contact
For questions or data deletion requests, email YOUR_EMAIL.
